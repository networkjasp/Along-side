Alongside is an independent consultancy built around a simple idea: understand the business, plan with the people involved and follow through on the work.

Clients work directly with Stephné, with continuity and oversight from start to finish.

When a brief needs more, Alongside draws on a trusted network of independent consultants and specialist businesses. This may include PR, website development, people and change communications, or professional editing. The right people are brought together for the right project.
