Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 2026

Alongside respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we look after it, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who we are

Alongside is a marketing and communications consultancy based in Pretoria, South Africa. The responsible party and Information Officer is Stephné du Toit-Smit, who can be reached at stephne@alongside.co.za or 084 587 9933.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, organisation, email address, phone number, the services you are interested in and your message. If you email, phone or WhatsApp us directly, we receive the details you share through that channel.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and our fonts and scripts are served from our own site rather than third-party networks.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss possible work with you.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who processes it

Form submissions are received and stored by our website host, Netlify, Inc., which may store data on servers outside South Africa. We use providers that are bound by data-protection obligations that provide a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA. Images on this site are served from Wix's media service (static.wixstatic.com), which receives standard technical information such as your IP address when the images load.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as we need it to respond and to manage any resulting work, after which it is deleted, unless we are required by law to keep it longer.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our processing it. Contact us at stephne@alongside.co.za. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za (opens in a new tab).

Security

This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us.
